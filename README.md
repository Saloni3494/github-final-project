# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple, intuitive, and lightweight tool to calculate simple interest and total accrued amount based on principal, annual rate of interest, and time period.

---

## 📌 Overview

Simple interest is a quick and easy method of calculating the interest charge on a loan or investment. It is determined by multiplying the daily or annual interest rate by the principal by the number of time periods that elapse.

### 📐 Mathematical Formula

The standard formula for calculating simple interest is:

$$\text{Simple Interest (SI)} = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}$$

Where:
- **$P$** = Principal Amount (initial amount borrowed or invested)
- **$R$** = Annual Rate of Interest (in percentage per annum)
- **$T$** = Time Period (in years)

The **Total Amount ($A$)** payable or receivable at maturity:

$$A = P + \text{SI}$$

---

## 💡 Example Calculation

Suppose you invest **$10,000** at an annual interest rate of **5%** for **3 years**:

- **Principal ($P$)**: $10,000
- **Rate ($R$)**: 5%
- **Time ($T$)**: 3 years

$$\text{SI} = \frac{10000 \times 5 \times 3}{100} = 1500$$

- **Simple Interest**: **$1,500**
- **Total Amount**: $10,000 + $1,500 = **$11,500**
